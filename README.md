# Starbucks — Five-Year Financial Model

A five-year financial model of Starbucks Corporation, built in Excel. The workbook projects the company's performance forward from its historical financials and lays out the assumptions behind each line.

## What's here

- **`Starbucks_5yr.xlsx`** — the model itself: historical financials, forward projections, and the driver assumptions behind revenue, margins, and cash flow.
- **`Harshit_Portfolio_Overview.docx`** — a short overview of my wider analytics and finance portfolio.

## What the model covers

- Revenue build and growth assumptions across the forecast period.
- Operating margin and cost structure.
- A projected income statement, with the key drivers isolated so they can be flexed.

## How to use it

Open `Starbucks_5yr.xlsx` in Excel. The assumption cells are the inputs; the statements update from them.
